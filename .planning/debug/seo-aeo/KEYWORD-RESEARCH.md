# Keyword Research & Search Demand Analysis — HandText

**Date:** September 2026  
**Product:** HandText  
**Primary Entity:** AI Handwriting Generator  

---

## 1. Methodology & Intent Classification

Keywords are clustered into distinct search intent categories based on actual user problem-solving goals:
1. **Commercial / Tool Intent (Core):** Users searching for a tool or software to generate handwriting from text.
2. **Academic Assignment Intent:** Students seeking to convert typed homework/assignments into handwritten sheets for submission.
3. **Study Notes Intent:** Users turning lecture transcripts, summaries, or typed notes into realistic handwritten notebooks.
4. **STEM / Technical Math Intent:** Users needing handwriting that supports LaTeX formulas, integrals, matrices, and mathematical notation.
5. **Format / Paper Layout Intent:** Users needing specific dimensions, margins, lined ruled paper, and multi-page A4 export.
